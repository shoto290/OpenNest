# Motion

## Easing

**`globals.css` declares no custom easing tokens.** The system uses the Tailwind defaults.

<EasingScale />

## Enter animations

Animations come from `tw-animate-css`, imported in `globals.css`. Utilities are composable: `animate-in` plus one or more of `zoom-in`, `spin-in`, `slide-in-from-*`, with `duration-*` and `ease-*` to tune them. The `animate-out` counterparts exist for exit transitions.

**Fades are for modals only.** A dialog and its backdrop fade and scale in — that is what says the page behind went away, and `settings-styles.ts` owns it for all of them. Everywhere else the system does not fade: popovers, menus, tooltips, rows and bubbles appear and leave outright, and what moves does so by transform or by clip. Outside a modal, reach for a transform, never for opacity.

<AnimationScale />

## Reduced motion

**`globals.css` declares no `prefers-reduced-motion` block.** Honouring the preference is the component's job: pair every `animate-*` or `transition-*` utility with its `motion-reduce:` counterpart, as the previews on this page do.

```tsx
<div className="animate-in zoom-in motion-reduce:animate-none" />
```
