# Typography

## Fonts

Two families, self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/roboto` and `@fontsource-variable/roboto-slab`, imported at the top of `src/styles/globals.css`. They are bound in the `@theme inline` block: `font-sans` is the default everywhere, `font-heading` is opt-in for headings.

```css
--font-sans: "Roboto Variable", sans-serif;
--font-heading: "Roboto Slab Variable", serif;
```
