# Scrollbars

Every scroll container in the app wears the same bar — the rules live on the bare pseudo-elements in the `@layer base` block of `globals.css`, so nothing has to opt in.

The shape comes from one trick: the gutter is `1rem`, but the thumb carries a `5px` transparent border clipped to its content box, leaving a `6px` bar floating inside its own padding. The track stays transparent, so the pane's own surface shows through.

## Tokens

The bar is chrome, not content: the two tokens are neutral greys declared once for each scheme, like `--bot-avatar-ink` and `--diagram-scheme`.

## Opting out

`scrollbar-hide` is the one escape hatch, for a pane that hides its own overflow behind a mask a bar would sit on top of. It is a Tailwind utility declared alongside the base rules.

## Gecko

`scrollbar-width` and `scrollbar-color` are declared on `:root` behind `@supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar)`, for Storybook opened in Firefox — the app itself never runs on Gecko. Chromium and Safari read `scrollbar-color` ahead of the pseudo-elements, so declaring it unconditionally would flatten the bar everywhere the app actually ships.
