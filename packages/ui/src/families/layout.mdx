# Layout

Shells and regions that hand room to their slots and draw nothing themselves. They define where a screen's parts sit; the parts come from every other family.

## Components

- **AppHeader** — the top region and its slots.
- **ThreadLayout** — transcript, composer and side context in one frame.
- **WorkspaceShell** — sidebar plus content, the outermost frame.
